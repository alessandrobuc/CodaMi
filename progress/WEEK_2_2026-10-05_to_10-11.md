# CodaMi: Weekly Progress
**Week 2: Mon 5 Oct to Sun 11 Oct 2026 (in progress, updated Fri 9 Oct)**

## Summary
This week the core features arrived: users can add their pets, report a lost or found pet, and contact each other. On top of that, the live Google map and notifications are now built, along with the client's feedback from the first review.

## What was built so far

### My Pets (Flow 2)
- Add, edit and delete pets.
- Each pet has a name, species (dog, cat or other), details and photos, with upload progress.
- Pet profile page with a shortcut: **"Report as lost"**.

### Lost and found reports (Flows 3 and 4)
- A guided form in six steps:
  1. Lost or found
  2. Which pet
  3. Where
  4. When
  5. Details and contact
  6. Review
- Lost: pick one of your pets. The photos and details are filled in automatically.
- Found: add photos and a description of the animal you found.
- Contact details are prefilled from the profile. The phone number is remembered for next time.
- A success screen after publishing, with a share option.

### Home screen
- **Lost** and **Found** tabs showing reports, newest first.
- Report cards with photo, time, area and description, plus pull to refresh.
- **New report** button.

### Report details
- Full report page with photos, place, time and description.
- Visitors can contact the owner with one tap: **Call**, **WhatsApp** or **Email**, and share the report.
- On your own report: your contact details are tappable (open the dialer or mail app, long press to copy), and you can mark the pet as found.

### Security
- Database and storage rules: only signed-in users can see reports, and only the owner can edit or close them.

### Client feedback (round 1), done on Wed 7 Oct
- **Found tab was empty after marking a pet as found.**
  - Fixed: the Found tab now has a **"Back home"** section showing pets that were reunited with their families.
- **Street field had no address suggestions.**
  - Fixed: typing now suggests streets and landmarks near the chosen city.
- **Exact address should not be public.**
  - Fixed: house numbers are removed and the location is blurred to an area of about **100 m**.
  - People see only "Near *street*, *city*".

### Live map, done on Thu 8 Oct
- **Google Maps** with a custom CodaMi style: soft colours, no shop clutter, photo pins that stand out.
- **Real time:** a new report appears on everyone's map within seconds, and a resolved one disappears.
- **Pins:** each pin shows the pet's photo, red for lost and green for found. Tap a pin to see the pet card, then open the full report.
- **Grouping:** when many pets are close together, they merge into a bubble with a count. Tap it to zoom in.
- **Privacy:** pets are shown as an area of about 100 m, never the exact spot.
- **Back home:** pets marked as found stay on the map as a green pin with a small home badge, under Found and All, so neighbours can see the happy endings.
- **Controls:** zoom in/out, My location, My city, and a full-screen map.
- **Filters:** Lost / Found / All, and distance from your city (1, 5, 10, 25 or 50 km, or everywhere).

### More accurate locations, done on Thu 8 Oct
- **Pin the spot:** when reporting, the user now places a pin on a map (drag the map or tap "Use my location"), so the report points to the exact place, not just the city.
- **Street name:** filled in automatically from the pin.
- **Wrong-city warning:** a warning appears if the pin is far from the chosen city.

### Notifications, done on Thu 8 Oct
- **Bell:** a bell on Home with an unread count, and a Notifications screen.
- **Alerts:** everyone in the city is alerted when a pet is lost or found there, and when a pet is back home.
- **Phone notifications:** notifications appear on the phone even when the app is closed. Tapping one opens the report.

### Home and lists, done on Thu 8 Oct
- **After publishing:** the app goes straight back to Home and shows the new pin.
- **More room for the map:** the header is cleaner, with the greeting and bell on one line.
- **Report button:** a new **"Report a pet"** button.
- **Lists:** they load 20 reports at a time and keep loading as you scroll, so they stay fast as the app grows.
- **Large numbers:** they are shortened (1.2k, 300+) so the layout never breaks.

### My reports, account deletion and translation, done on Fri 9 Oct
- **My reports:** a new screen in the profile lists your own reports in two tabs, **Active** and **Resolved**.
  - From each report you can mark the pet as found or delete the report.
  - The owner can also delete a report from its full page.
- **Delete account:** a new option in the profile, required by Google Play and the App Store.
  - It permanently deletes your profile, pets, reports and photos.
  - A warning appears first.
- **Italian translation:** the app now follows the phone's language (Italian or English, Italian by default).
  - Translated so far: the bottom bar, Profile, My reports and the report dialogs.
  - The rest of the app follows next.
- **10 languages ready:** Italian, English, Spanish, French, German, Portuguese, Russian, Arabic, Chinese and Hindi.
  - A new **Language** option in the profile shows each language with its flag, so users can pick one or follow the phone.
  - Languages other than Italian and English show English until they are translated next week.
- **Photo fix:** deleting a pet no longer removes the photos used by its lost report.
- **Release build:** installed on a real phone for testing. We're also checking why sign-up felt slow in the test build.
