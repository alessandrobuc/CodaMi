# CodaMi: Weekly Progress
**Week 4: Mon 19 Oct to Sun 25 Oct 2026 (in progress, updated Fri 23 Oct, evening)**

## Summary
Users can now choose their own notification sounds, and a new Videos tab shows motivational and educational videos picked for the animals they own. Admins upload and manage those videos from the admin side. On Tuesday the full support system was built: live chat and requests for users, and a Tickets section with operator monitoring for the owner. A new Android build with all of this goes to Google Play closed testing.

## What was built so far

### Client clarifications, received Mon 19 Oct
The client explained three requests in more detail: customizable notifications, random videos based on the user's animals, and live chat with full monitoring of the support staff. The approach and the open questions were sent back to the client, and `PHASE_2_REQUESTS.md` was updated. Videos and notifications were done first. Support comes next, after the client's answers.

### Notification settings, Mon 19 Oct
- **Profile → Notifications** has three alert types:
  - **Lost pets nearby**
  - **Found pets nearby**
  - **Happy endings** (a pet back home)
- Each type has an on/off switch and its own sound, so people can tell what happened without looking.
- **Sounds:**
  - the phone's default, silent, or four CodaMi sounds (Chime, Bell, Bright, Soft)
  - tapping a sound plays a preview
- **Android:** users can also pick any ringtone or tone on the phone, or any music or audio file.
- **iPhone:** the four CodaMi sounds. Apple only allows sounds that are stored inside the app, so ringtones and music can't be used.
- **Server:** each alert is sent with the user's chosen sound. Turned-off types send no phone alert but still appear in the app's notifications list. Older app versions keep working as before.

### Videos, Mon 19 Oct
- **New Videos tab** in the bottom bar (Home, Pets, Videos, Profile):
  - a large featured video, then a list, with All / Motivation / Learn filters
  - most are about the animals the user has registered (dog, cat, other), with some general animal-welfare videos mixed in
  - users without pets see only the general videos
- The mix changes every time the app opens. A shuffle button (or pulling down) shows others. Videos already watched move to the end and are marked "Watched".
- Videos in the user's language come first. While no videos are uploaded, the tab says videos are on the way.
- **Video player:**
  - controls that hide on their own, and a spinner while the video loads or buffers and after jumping to another point
  - back / forward 10 seconds
  - a seek bar that shows how much has loaded
  - full screen, and a Try again button if the connection fails
- **Admin side → More → Videos (admins only):**
  - upload a video from the gallery (up to 200 MB), with an optional cover image
  - set the title, the type (Motivation or Learn), the animals (dog, cat, other or everyone) and the language
  - hide or show, edit, or delete (the files are removed too)
  - a simple list (All / Visible / Hidden filters, a "Hidden" badge on hidden videos) and an upload form with a preview of the chosen video
  - the cover image can be added, changed or removed later when editing a video
- Only admins can upload or change videos. Users only see visible ones.

### Fixes and polish, Mon 19 Oct
- **Phone status bar:** after full-screen video the phone's top and bottom bars stayed hidden and showed a black strip at the top. The app now brings the bars back when leaving full screen and every time it starts, and uses the area next to the camera notch.
- **Bottom navigation bar** (user app and admin side) now always keeps a small gap above the phone's own navigation buttons.
- The **Add video** button stays clear of the phone's navigation buttons.
- Fixed a crash when opening the Videos tab.
- Thu 22 Oct, emulator test of the admin side as a support account: a request's Details no longer has a large empty gap under the time cards (same fix on the new emergency, new report and video form screens), and the user's name and email now line up. Signing out of the admin side now sets the operator offline first and no longer causes an error, and support staff see "Support team" (not "Admin") on messages from other operators. The Emergency settings and Points and rewards screens were redesigned: a header, one card per section, each setting on its own row with a small number box, and extra space at the end.

### Live chat and support requests, Tue 20 Oct
- **Profile → Help & support:**
  - **Start a live chat**, available while at least one operator is online (a green dot shows it). Otherwise users are asked to **send a request**.
  - pick a topic (account, a listing, the app, something else), write a subject and a message
  - **My requests:** every conversation with its reference number (for example `CM-1042`), status and last message
  - a chat screen with the full history. Users can mark a request as solved; writing again reopens it. Closed requests can't be written to.
  - a push notification when support replies (opens the conversation). It has its own row in Profile → Notifications, with its own sound.
  - at most 3 open requests per user, to prevent spam
- **Admin side → Tickets** (admins and support staff):
  - filters: **Unanswered** (oldest first), **Unassigned**, **Mine**, **Active**, **Finished**
  - search by reference number or user email
  - each request shows its status, the operator, how long the user has been waiting, and a red border when the reply is late
  - an **Available for live chat** switch, so operators can go "away"
- **Request screen (staff):**
  - **Chat:** an operator must **take the case** before replying, so every request has one clear owner. Two operators can't take the same case. Admins can always reply.
  - **Details:** user, operator, topic, opened, taken over, wait before takeover, first reply, average reply time, time spent, resolved and closed times, number of messages
  - **Timeline:** every action with who did it and when (opened, taken, reassigned, status changes, reopened, late reply, closed automatically). It cannot be edited.
  - status: open, in progress, waiting for response, resolved, closed. Admins can **reassign** a case to another operator or put it back in the queue.
- **Admin side → More → Operators** (admins only):
  - each operator's status (online, away, offline) and last activity
  - open cases, cases taken and resolved, replies, average reply time and average time per case
  - opening an operator shows all their cases
- **Dashboard:** unanswered requests, unassigned requests and late replies
- **Server:**
  - times are recorded by the server, so operators can't change them
  - every 10 minutes the server checks for requests with no reply in time (5 minutes for a chat, 24 hours for a request) and sends the admins a notification
  - requests marked as resolved close by themselves after 7 days
  - these limits can be changed in the database (`config/support`) without a new app version
  - deleting an account also deletes that user's support requests
- Staff now get push notifications on the admin side too (new chats, new messages on their cases, late replies).

### Adding support staff, Tue 20 Oct
- **Support staff → Add** now opens the list of users (newest first, with Load more) instead of asking for an exact email. Tap a person, confirm, and they join the team. People who are already staff or admin, and disabled accounts, can't be picked.
- **Users → open a user** has a **Support staff** switch to add or remove them directly.
- **Search** in both lists finds users by the start of their name or email (for example "mar" finds Mario Rossi). The server keeps a lowercase copy of each name and email for this; existing users get it the next time they open the app, and the full email always works.
- When someone is removed from the team (or their account is deleted), their open cases go back to the queue automatically, and the timeline records it.
- Admins are still created only in the Firestore console. The app can only switch other users between user and support staff, and the database rules enforce it.
- **Design:** the Tickets tab now has a summary line ("3 waiting for a reply" / "All caught up"), an Online / Away button, and three count cards (Unanswered, Unassigned, Late) that also work as filters. Each ticket card has a coloured edge for its status, the user's initial, the subject, the last message and pills for the operator and waiting time. The request screen has a header with the user, status, operator and waiting time and a one-tap Take / Mark as resolved button; the chat has day separators, grouped bubbles and a new message bar; Details shows the four key times as cards and the rest in clear sections; the timeline is a set of cards on a line.
- **Chat pages:** the chat loads the newest 30 messages and loads older ones while scrolling up (or with "Load earlier messages").
- **Notifications while the app is open:** phones don't show a push while the app is in front, so the app now shows its own banner with an Open button (not for the chat already on screen). This matters most for operators, who must have the app open to be online.
- **No operator online:** new requests and messages on unassigned requests now notify every staff member instead of nobody.
- **Every support event now sends a notification** (all in 10 languages, tapping opens the request, nobody is notified about their own action):

  | Event | Who is notified |
  |---|---|
  | New chat or request | Online operators (all staff if nobody is online) |
  | User writes | The assigned operator (online operators / all staff if unassigned) |
  | Staff replies | The user |
  | An operator takes the request | The user ("Your request is being handled") |
  | Admin assigns or reassigns | The new operator |
  | Staff marks resolved or closed | The user |
  | User marks it solved | The assigned operator |
  | Reply is late | The assigned operator and all admins |
  | A removed staff member's cases go back to the queue | Online operators (all staff if nobody is online) |

  Status changes to "in progress" or "waiting for response" and the automatic close after 7 days send nothing, to avoid spam.
- Someone removed from the team no longer counts as "online" for live chat.

### Emergencies on the map, Fri 23 Oct
- Every active emergency now shows on the map as a **red pin** with an icon for its type (injured, in danger, abandoned...). The pins are always visible, whichever filter is on (All / Lost / Found), and sit above the pet pins.
- Tapping a pin shows a card with the type, the place, how long ago it was reported and its status. Tapping the card opens the emergency.

### Invite code filled in automatically on Android, Fri 23 Oct
- When a friend installs CodaMi from the shared invite link, the Play Store passes the code to the app. The "Did a friend invite you?" screen now fills it in by itself, with a note that it came from the invite link. The friend only taps **Use** to confirm, or can change or skip it.

### Google sign-in fix, Fri 23 Oct
- Google sign-in failed in the Play Store version ("something went wrong") but worked when installed from the computer. The cause: when the store version is built, Android removes files it thinks are unused to make the app smaller, and it removed the Google sign-in ID. It is now kept. The fix goes out with the next build.

### What's new, feedback and app rating, Fri 23 Oct
The client asked for a review popup with reward points and for patch notes after each update.
- **What's new:** after an update, users see a page with what's new, improved and fixed in that version, once per version. They can see it again in Profile → What's new. Admins write the notes in Admin → More → What's new, per version and per language (English is used where a language is missing), and can keep them as a draft until ready. A new install doesn't show them.
  - Notes appear in the language the user chose in the app and switch with it.
  - In Profile → What's new, the newest version is open and older ones are closed cards with a short summary (for example "2 New · 1 Fixed"); tap to open. Versions load 10 at a time with "Load more", in the admin list too.
  - Low cost: each version is a single record with all languages inside, and the app only checks for notes once after each update, not on every start.
- **Rating and reviews:** Google Play and the App Store don't allow giving points for ratings or reviews, so:
  - users are asked for **feedback inside the app** (1 to 5 stars and a comment). The first feedback gives **20 points**, once per account, and only after a few days of use. The team reads it in Admin → More → Feedback, with the average rating.
  - the official **Google Play / Apple rating window** appears separately, without points, after 10 days and some real use, at most every 4 months.
  - neither appears right after sign-up, nor while the user is doing something: only 25 seconds after opening the app, on the Home screen, with nothing else open. Profile also has **Send feedback** and **Rate CodaMi**.
- All the numbers (points, days, how often) can be changed in Admin → More → Points and rewards.
- Translated into all 10 languages. 15 new tests and 25 new emulator checks pass; all existing tests still pass.
- Tested on the phone (admin side): adding a version, wrong and duplicate version numbers refused, English required, publishing, the new settings and the Feedback screen.

### Invite code after sign-up, Thu 22 Oct
- New users now see a short **"Did a friend invite you?"** screen right after choosing their city, so invited friends don't miss the code. It shows once, has a Skip button, and never shows for existing users, staff or people who already used a code. The box in Profile → Invite friends still works for 7 days.
- The code box is smarter: lowercase, spaces and dashes are fixed automatically, and pasting the whole invite message or link keeps just the code.
- Each problem has its own clear message: wrong format, code not found, your own code, too late (more than 7 days after sign-up), code already used, too many tries.
- To stop people guessing random codes, the server allows 10 wrong codes per day per account.
