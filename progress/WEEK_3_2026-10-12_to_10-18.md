# CodaMi: Weekly Progress
**Week 3: Mon 12 Oct to Sun 18 Oct 2026 (in progress, updated Mon 12 Oct)**

## Summary
CodaMi now speaks 10 languages. Every screen, message and phone notification is translated, and users can switch language at any time, even before signing up.

## What was built so far

### The whole app in 10 languages, done on Mon 12 Oct
- **Languages:** English, Italian, Spanish, French, German, Portuguese, Russian, Arabic, Chinese and Hindi.
- **Everything is translated:**
  - onboarding, sign up and log in
  - city setup, home, map, lists, report form and report pages
  - pets, profile, notifications, error messages
  - share text and the WhatsApp / email messages
- **Dates and times** follow the language too (for example "vor 2 Tagen", "2 h fa", "Fr 9. Okt.").
- **English by default:** the app starts in English, and users choose their language themselves.
- **Language button on the first screens:** a flag button on the first onboarding screen and on the log in / sign up screen opens the language list.
- **Profile → Language:** the same list, available any time after signing in.
- **Arabic:** the layout flips right to left. The log in / sign up switch was fixed for this.

### Notifications in each user's language
- Each user's chosen language is saved to their profile.
- Phone alerts ("Lost pet in Milano", "Luna is back home") are sent in each person's own language.
- The notifications list in the app follows the current app language.

### Privacy Policy, Terms of Use and safer account deletion, done on Mon 12 Oct
- **Privacy Policy** and **Terms of Use** pages, opened from the profile.
  - Written for how CodaMi actually works: what data is stored, the 100 m location blur, Google/Firebase as providers, GDPR rights and in-app account deletion.
  - Available in English and Italian. The other languages show the English text with a short translated note.
  - CodaMi is named as the owner. The email is never shown in the text: a **Contact us** button at the bottom of each page opens the mail app.
  - Still waiting for the support email from the client.
- **Delete account now asks for proof:**
  - Email accounts must enter their password, and it is checked with Firebase.
  - Google and Apple accounts must type CONFIRM.
  - The server also refuses deletion if an email account has not just re-entered its password.
