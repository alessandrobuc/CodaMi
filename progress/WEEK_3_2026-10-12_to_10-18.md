# CodaMi: Weekly Progress
**Week 3: Mon 12 Oct to Sun 18 Oct 2026 (in progress, updated Thu 15 Oct, afternoon)**

## Summary
CodaMi now speaks 10 languages. Every screen, message and phone notification is translated, and users can switch language at any time, even before signing up.

## What was built so far

### The whole app in 10 languages, done on Mon 12 Oct
- **Languages:** English, Italian, Spanish, French, German, Portuguese, Russian, Arabic, Chinese and Hindi.
- **Everything is translated:**
  - onboarding, sign up and log in
  - city setup, home, map, lists, report form and report pages
  - pets, profile, notifications, error messages
  - share text and the WhatsApp / email messages
- **Dates and times** follow the language too (for example "vor 2 Tagen", "2 h fa", "Fr 9. Okt.").
- **English by default:** the app starts in English, and users choose their language themselves.
- **Language button on the first screens:** a flag button on the first onboarding screen and on the log in / sign up screen opens the language list.
- **Profile → Language:** the same list, available any time after signing in.
- **Arabic:** the layout flips right to left. The log in / sign up switch was fixed for this.

### Notifications in each user's language
- Each user's chosen language is saved to their profile.
- Phone alerts ("Lost pet in Milano", "Luna is back home") are sent in each person's own language.
- The notifications list in the app follows the current app language.

### Privacy Policy, Terms of Use and safer account deletion, done on Mon 12 Oct
- **Privacy Policy** and **Terms of Use** pages, opened from the profile.
  - Written for how CodaMi actually works: what data is stored, the 100 m location blur, Google/Firebase as providers, GDPR rights and in-app account deletion.
  - Available in English and Italian. The other languages show the English text with a short translated note.
  - CodaMi is named as the owner. The email is never shown in the text: a **Contact us** button at the bottom of each page opens the mail app.
  - Still waiting for the support email from the client.
- **Delete account now asks for proof:**
  - Email accounts must enter their password, and it is checked with Firebase.
  - Google and Apple accounts must type CONFIRM.
  - The server also refuses deletion if an email account has not just re-entered its password.

### Getting ready for Google Play, done on Wed 14 Oct
- **First build uploaded** to Google Play for closed testing.
- **Release signing:** the app is now signed with its own upload key instead of the test key. Google Sign-In is set up for the version installed from Play.
- **Public Privacy Policy page** (`docs/index.html`), ready for GitHub Pages, with step-by-step account deletion.
- **Store graphics:** feature graphic, 512 px icon and four framed screenshots with captions (`assets/graphics/`).
- **Store texts:** app name, short and full description in English and Italian.
- **Play Console forms:** content rating, target audience (18 and over) and Data safety answers.
- **Fixed a Play error:** a location plugin added a background location permission the app never uses. It is now removed, so Play no longer asks about it.
- **Fixed:** tapping **Continue with Google** (or Apple) showed the loading spinner on the Log in / Sign up button. The spinner now appears on the button that was tapped.

### Thu 15 Oct
- **Language screen on first launch:** the first time CodaMi opens, a full screen shows all 10 languages with a Continue button. Tapping a language switches the app right away. It is only asked once, and it can still be changed in Profile → Language.
- **Optional reward on lost listings:** owners can add a reward in euros when reporting a lost pet. It shows on the listing and the report page with a note that it is a private arrangement and CodaMi does not guarantee or process the payment. It disappears automatically when the pet is marked as found.
- **Admin side:** after login the app checks each person's role. Admins and support staff go to a separate admin side with its own navigation (Dashboard, Listings, Reported, Tickets, More); ordinary users never see it. The dashboard shows live numbers (users, open lost and found reports, pets back home). Admins can add and remove support staff by email. Roles are protected on the server, so nobody can make themselves an admin. Listings, reported content, tickets, users and videos are next.

The language work is mostly done already; the rest is new. Status, approach and open questions are in `PHASE_2_REQUESTS.md`.
