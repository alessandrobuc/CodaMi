# CodaMi: Weekly Progress
**Week 1: Mon 28 Sep to Sun 4 Oct 2026**

## Summary
This week laid the foundation. The project is set up and connected to Firebase, and a new user can install the app, see the onboarding, create an account, choose their home city and land on the main screen.

## What was built

### Project setup
- Created the CodaMi Flutter app (Android and iOS) with a clean, feature-based structure.
- Connected Firebase (project `codami-cd91e`): Authentication, Firestore database, Storage and Cloud Functions.

### Branding
- App icon for Android and iOS.
- Splash screen with the CodaMi logo and name.
- Brand colours and fonts applied across the app.

### Onboarding
- Welcome screens that introduce CodaMi before sign-up.

### Accounts
- Sign up, log in and forgot password, with field validation and clear error messages.
- A note telling users to check their spam folder for the reset email.
- Users stay logged in and land on the right screen when they reopen the app.

### Home city setup (Flow 1)
- After sign-up, the user searches for and picks their home city.
- City search uses Google Places, focused on Italy and shown in Italian. The search runs on our server, so the Google key is never inside the app.
- The chosen city is saved to the user's profile.

### Main navigation
- Animated bottom bar with three tabs: Home, Pets and Profile.
- Profile shows the user's real name from their account.

## Status at end of week
- Install → onboarding → sign up → choose city → main screen works end to end.
