# CodaMi: What We Need From You

_Fri 23 Oct 2026_

These are needed before the app can be published. Items 1 to 4 are the most urgent.

---

## Urgent (blocking the release)

### 1. Support email address
The email address users can write to. It goes in the Privacy Policy, the Terms of Service, the public privacy page and the store listings. Google and Apple require it.

### 2. Apple Developer account access
Please add us to your Apple Developer account and App Store Connect. We need it to:
- test the app on a real iPhone
- turn on iPhone notifications
- finish Sign in with Apple
- submit the app to the App Store

### 3. Email service for emergency alerts
To email emergency alerts to vets and associations, we need an email sending service, for example **Brevo** or **SendGrid** (both have free plans).
- Is it OK to set one up?
- Which sender address should the emails come from (for example `alerts@yourdomain.com`)?

Until then, users can see the organizations in the app and call or email them directly.

### 4. Real emergency contacts
The app currently shows test contacts, clearly marked as tests. For each real vet, association or service, please send:
- name and type (vet, association, rescue service, ...)
- phone number(s) and email
- address and the area they cover
- which emergencies they handle
- opening hours (or 24/7)
- confirmation that they agree to be listed in the app

---

## Decisions

### 5. Donations: yes or no?
If yes, we need:
- the amounts (for example €1 / €3 / €5 / €10 / €25)
- a payments profile in Google Play Console and the Paid Apps agreement in App Store Connect (tax and bank details, these must be done by you)
- a RevenueCat account with access for us (free until about $2,500 a month in donations, then about 1%)

### 6. Support team
- Who will answer chats and requests?
- Which hours and which languages?
- Are these time limits OK: a reply within **5 minutes** for live chat, within **24 hours** for requests, and resolved requests close after **7 days**? (They can be changed at any time without a new app version.)
- Do you want users to be able to send photos in the support chat?

### 7. Videos
- A first set of videos (dogs, cats and general animal welfare) that you have the rights to use.
- Should we also support YouTube links, or only uploaded videos?

### 8. iPhone notification sounds
Apple only allows sounds stored inside the app. On iPhone, users can choose from the 4 CodaMi sounds but not their own ringtones or music (Android allows both). Is this OK?

### 9. Review popup and points
Google Play and the App Store don't allow giving points for ratings or reviews; apps that do can be removed. Is it OK to:
- show the review popup **without points**, after the user has used the app for a while, and
- give points for a short **feedback form inside the app** instead (once per user)?

---

## Before launch

### 10. Translation review
A native speaker should check the app texts, Italian first.

### 11. Android signing key
Please confirm you keep a safe copy of the Android signing key and its passwords. If it is lost, the app can no longer be updated on Google Play.
