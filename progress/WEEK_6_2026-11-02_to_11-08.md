## What was built so far

### New requests, Mon 2 Nov
While waiting for the Stripe details, three new requests from the client were planned and built the same day.

**Invitations with QR codes**
- Every user has a personal QR code on the Invite friends screen, next to the invite code.
- **QR code and flyer:** the QR alone, or a ready flyer with the CodaMi logo, a short invitation, the QR and the code. Both can be saved to the phone's photos or shared, to print and hand out in the neighbourhood.
- **One QR for Android and iPhone:** it opens a small CodaMi invite page. On Android it goes straight to Google Play with the code attached (filled in automatically); on iPhone it shows the code to type in and the App Store button (once the app is on the App Store). The page speaks the visitor's language.
- Friends who join through the QR count exactly like normal invites.

**Places to walk with pets**
- Google finds dog parks, parks, green areas and gardens, trails and nature parks around the user automatically; nothing has to be added by hand.
- Map with coloured pins or a list, filters by type, distance of 1, 3, 5, 10 or 25 km, near the user's position or their city, and "Search this area" after moving the map.
- Each place: name, address, distance; when opened, a photo, Google rating, open now, opening hours, "dogs allowed" when Google knows it, Directions on foot, call and website.
- Admin → More → Places to walk: on/off, default distance, place types, daily limits per user (to keep Google costs under control), and places hidden by an admin.
- Searches go through our server, so the Google key is never in the app. Starts switched off.

**Voluntary donations (screens ready, payments later)**
- A **Support CodaMi** page explaining what the support pays for (servers, development, moderation, support, translations, new features, awareness campaigns), with the amounts.
- A gentle message after a report is published and when a pet is found, never before and at most once a month (adjustable), always with "Not now"; plus a row in Profile.
- Admin → More → Support CodaMi: on/off, each place on/off, how often, amounts, preview.
- Payments go through Google Play and the App Store; until the store setup is done, the button says payments are coming soon. It starts switched off.

All three are translated into the 10 languages and covered by new tests (115 app tests, 75 server tests and the security checks all pass).

### Invite tracking, Tue 3 Nov
Your questions answered: the invite code is permanent (one per user, never changes; the QR and the link carry the same code). Tracking was planned and built the same day.

- **A status for every invite:** **Active** (the friend really started using CodaMi and the inviter got the points), **Inactive** (didn't become active within 14 days, or rejected as suspicious) and **Waiting** (still has time). Plus **Still using the app**: active friends who opened the app in the last 30 days (changeable in Points and rewards).
- **QR and link tracking:** the invite page now counts QR scans and shared-link opens separately, once a day per visitor. Only numbers are saved, nothing personal about the visitor. Scans are counted from this update onward; all earlier invites are already counted.
- **Admin → Users → a user:** a new **Invites** box with the invite code, QR scans, link opens, sign-ups, active, still using, inactive and waiting, and **See invited friends** with each friend's status, sign-up date and last time in the app.
- **Admin → More → Invites:** totals for the whole app and a ranking of users by most active or most invited, for all time, this month, last month or chosen dates. Ready for future activities and contests.
- **Invite friends screen:** each user sees their own Active / Inactive / Waiting counts.
- Translated into the 10 languages and covered by new tests (125 app tests, 80 server tests and 323 end-to-end checks all pass).
- Online and tested on the phone together with Monday's work (QR invite page, places to walk).
- The Google Places key is moved to a protected server secret (safer, ready for launch); the map keys for Android and iPhone are restricted to the app.
- The count boxes line up: boxes in the same row are the same height, with Active / Inactive / Waiting together in one row.

### AI breed scanner, Wed 4 Nov
- **Take or choose a photo** of a dog, cat or other pet, with tips for a good photo.
- **Result:** the most likely breed with a percentage and a bar, how sure the AI is, other possible breeds with their percentages, mixed breeds shown as a mix, a warning when the photo isn't clear, and facts about the breed (size, weight, life span, temperament, characteristics, care), all in the user's language.
- Always says clearly that it's an AI estimate, not a guarantee. **The photo is not saved.**
- Easy to find: a **"Which breed is it?" card at the top of the Pets tab**, plus Profile → Breed scanner.
- **Save breed to my pet** from the result, or **Find the breed with AI** while adding a pet.
- **Admin → More → Breed scanner:** on/off, scans per user per day (10), and a button to try it. The **AI model is picked from a list** that comes live from Google, so new Gemini models appear by themselves; each one says if it's the cheapest, fast and accurate, or the most accurate, and there's a warning if Google retires the chosen one.
- Uses Google's newest Gemini model; each scan costs less than 1 cent. 10 languages, fully tested. Online, and the first scan on the phone works.

### Heat calendar, Wed 4 Nov
- On every female pet's page, a **Heat calendar** card: "in heat now, day 4" or "next heat expected around 28 Nov".
- **Record each heat** (start, end or still in heat, notes) and keep the full history; edit or delete any entry.
- **Estimates:** the next heats are worked out from the pet's own cycles; until there are two records, the typical cycle for dogs, cats or other animals is used. A month calendar shows past heats in full colour and expected ones lighter.
- **Reminders** 1, 3, 7 or 14 days before the expected heat, as a notification in the owner's language, even if the app isn't opened.
- **Admin → More → Heat calendar:** on/off and the typical values per species.
- Private: only the owner sees their pets' heats. 10 languages, fully tested; to put online and test on the phone.

### Pet sitting, Wed 4 Nov
- **Pet sitting section** in the Pets tab and in Profile: requests near you, sitters near you, and your own requests, profile and offers, with distance and animal filters.
- **Owners** publish a request (pets, service, dates, details, optional budget). Only the city is shown, never an address.
- **Sitters** create a profile (about, experience, animals, services, availability, distance) and send offers. The owner accepts one; the others are declined automatically.
- **Chat** between owner and sitter inside the app, with notifications and an unread count. Report and block in every chat. Long chats load a page at a time as you scroll up, so they open quickly.
- After the first test on the phone: buttons now show a loading spinner while they work, accepting an offer is faster, and the first letter of the name shows correctly in the chat avatar.
- **Verified reviews:** after the dates, both sides confirm the service; only then the owner can leave one review. New accounts have to wait a few days, and ratings are calculated by the server, so they can't be faked.
- Fixed Thu 5 Nov: sending a review was always refused, because every account looked brand new to the server; the account age now comes from the sign-in record. The review sheet also has more space under the Send button. In Admin → Pet sitting → Reported, "View profile" now opens the user's admin page, so it works for owners too (before it showed "Not found" when the reported person had no sitter profile).
- **Admin → More → Pet sitting:** on/off, reported items, requests, sitters and reviews, each can be hidden.
- A friendly look in CodaMi's own colours: a green welcome card, calendar-style dates, a progress line from "published" to "reviewed", sitter profiles with a banner and their stats, and chat bubbles like a messaging app.
- Safety tips in the section and in every chat; payment is arranged between the users. 10 languages, fully tested; to put online and test on two phones.

### Personal schedule, Thu 5 Nov
- **My schedule** for every user: a card at the top of the Pets tab with the next event, a card on each pet's page, and a row in Profile.
- **Events:** vet visit, vaccination, medication, grooming, feeding, walk or activity, note and other, for one or more pets or none. Date, time or all day, place, vaccine or medicine and dose, notes.
- **Repeat:** every day, week (chosen days, for example Mon–Fri), month or year, or every few days or weeks; ends never, on a date or after a number of times.
- **Reminders:** up to 2 per event, as push notifications even with the app closed, in the user's language. Defaults as agreed: vet, vaccination and grooming 1 day and 1 hour before; medication and feeding at the time; walks 15 minutes before. Tapping one opens the event.
- **Calendar, Upcoming and Done** views, filter by pet and type; expected heats from the heat calendar show as estimates.
- **Mark done, skip once, edit or delete** only this time or all times. Missed items from the last week show as "Not done yet".
- **Private:** only the user sees it, not even admins; removed with the account. Deleting a pet asks whether to delete its events too.
- Admin → More → My schedule: on/off. 10 languages, fully tested; to put online and test on the phone.

### Social sharing, Thu 5 Nov
- **Share pop-up** right after publishing a lost or found report, and when a pet is back home ("Share the happy news! 🎉"). "Not now" always there; the Share button on every report opens the same options.
- **Share image** made on the phone in CodaMi's colours: the pet's photo, a big LOST / FOUND / BACK HOME label, name, species, area, date, reward, the CodaMi logo and a **QR code** to the report, so it also works as a printed poster.
- **One tap per app:** WhatsApp (text + link with a photo preview), Facebook (link card), Instagram and TikTok (the image, with the caption and hashtags copied to paste), plus the phone's share menu, Save image and Copy link.
- **Public report page** for each report: photo, details, "Seen Luna? Open CodaMi", store buttons and **Open in CodaMi**, which opens the report straight in the app. WhatsApp and Facebook show it as a preview card with the photo. No phone, email or exact address; gone when the report is removed, a "back home" card when resolved.
- **Numbers:** owners see "Shared 12 times · 40 page visits" on their report; Admin → More → Social sharing shows the last 30 days by app and where visits came from, and switches the pop-up on or off.
- 10 languages, fully tested; to put online and test on the phone.
