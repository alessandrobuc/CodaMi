## What was built so far

### New requests, Mon 2 Nov
While waiting for the Stripe details, three new requests from the client were planned and built the same day.

**Invitations with QR codes**
- Every user has a personal QR code on the Invite friends screen, next to the invite code.
- **QR code and flyer:** the QR alone, or a ready flyer with the CodaMi logo, a short invitation, the QR and the code. Both can be saved to the phone's photos or shared, to print and hand out in the neighbourhood.
- **One QR for Android and iPhone:** it opens a small CodaMi invite page. On Android it goes straight to Google Play with the code attached (filled in automatically); on iPhone it shows the code to type in and the App Store button (once the app is on the App Store). The page speaks the visitor's language.
- Friends who join through the QR count exactly like normal invites.

**Places to walk with pets**
- Google finds dog parks, parks, green areas and gardens, trails and nature parks around the user automatically; nothing has to be added by hand.
- Map with coloured pins or a list, filters by type, distance of 1, 3, 5, 10 or 25 km, near the user's position or their city, and "Search this area" after moving the map.
- Each place: name, address, distance; when opened, a photo, Google rating, open now, opening hours, "dogs allowed" when Google knows it, Directions on foot, call and website.
- Admin → More → Places to walk: on/off, default distance, place types, daily limits per user (to keep Google costs under control), and places hidden by an admin.
- Searches go through our server, so the Google key is never in the app. Starts switched off.

**Voluntary donations (screens ready, payments later)**
- A **Support CodaMi** page explaining what the support pays for (servers, development, moderation, support, translations, new features, awareness campaigns), with the amounts.
- A gentle message after a report is published and when a pet is found, never before and at most once a month (adjustable), always with "Not now"; plus a row in Profile.
- Admin → More → Support CodaMi: on/off, each place on/off, how often, amounts, preview.
- Payments go through Google Play and the App Store; until the store setup is done, the button says payments are coming soon. It starts switched off.

All three are translated into the 10 languages and covered by new tests (115 app tests, 75 server tests and the security checks all pass).

## Plan for the week

### Shop day 3: the order system (once the Stripe keys and details arrive, aimed for Tue 3 Nov)
- **Checkout screen:** delivery details (name, address, postcode, city, province, phone, delivery notes), saved for next time, and a review of the order with products, shipping method and total with VAT.
- **Orders saved on the server:** prices, availability and shipping are checked again by the server, so the app can't change them. Each order line keeps its price, cost, margin and commission at the time of sale. An order with products from several suppliers is split into one shipment per supplier.
- **My orders:** list, details, a status timeline, a tracking link once shipped, and a notification at each step.
- **Admin → Shop → Orders:** filter by status, order details and address, change the status, add the courier and tracking number for each shipment, internal notes.
- **Supplier emails for dropshipping:** prepared, and sent once the email service is set up.
- **Payment with Stripe:** cards, Apple Pay and Google Pay at checkout, and refunds from the admin panel.
- The shop stays off for users until payment is tested.
